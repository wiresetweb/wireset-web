✦ A note from Alexander

Cooked the way Abuela cooked it.

I named the place for my grandmother Lupita — she's the one who taught me, in her kitchen in Veracruz, the long way. Pork shoulder gets eight hours. Masa is pressed by hand every morning. Salsas are made from whole peppers we char on the grill, not from a jar. Nothing here came out of a freezer or a sysco truck.

We opened in 2019 with two picnic tables and a portable trompo. Five years later we're still on the same block, still making it her way. If you've never had a tortilla pressed five minutes ago, that's why you should come.

— Alexander Reyes, founder & chef
