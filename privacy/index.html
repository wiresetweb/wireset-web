Privacy Policy

Plainly written, and shorter than most. We collect as little as possible, use privacy-first tools, and never sell your data.

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Wireset Web ("we") builds websites and custom web tools for local service businesses. This page explains what we collect when you visit wiresetweb.com or contact us, and how we use it. If anything here is unclear, email hello@wiresetweb.com and we'll answer plainly.

The short version

  • We use one advertising pixel (Meta) to measure our ads — and we honor the Global Privacy Control opt-out signal, so you can switch it off.
  • Our traffic analytics are cookieless and aggregated — they can't identify you.
  • The only personal details we collect are the ones you type into our contact form, and we use them only to reply.
  • We never sell your personal information.

Information you give us

When you submit a contact or quote form, you send us your name, business name, email, an optional phone number, and your message. That submission is delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, a form-handling service, and we use it solely to respond to you and, if you become a client, to do the work. We keep inquiries only as long as we need them to run the business, and you can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Information collected automatically

Cookieless traffic analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand general traffic — page views, the pages people visit, rough referrer and country, and how fast pages load. It is privacy-first: it uses no cookies, builds no profile of you, and does not track you across other websites. The data is aggregated and cannot identify you.

First-party campaign attribution

So we can tell which of our own links and ads are working, your browser stores a small first-party record (in localStorage, not a cookie) noting things like the campaign tag or referring site that first brought you here. This stays in your browser, is never shared with third parties or advertising networks, and is used only to measure our own marketing. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we don't store it at all. You can clear it any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Payments

If you pay a deposit or invoice, payment is handled by Stripe. Stripe processes your card details directly — we never see or store your full card number. See Stripe's privacy policy for how they handle payment data.

Cookies and advertising

We use the Meta (Facebook) pixel to measure our advertising and to reach people who have visited the site. It sets cookies and shares limited event data — such as page views and that a form was submitted — with Meta. We do not send Meta your name, email, phone, or message contents.

How to opt out: we honor the browser Global Privacy Control signal — if it's on, the pixel does not load at all. You can also block it with your browser's cookie settings or an ad blocker, or adjust your Meta ad preferences.

Who we share data with

We don't sell your data. We share it only with the service providers that make the site run, each acting on our behalf:

  • Cloudflare — hosting and cookieless analytics
  • Web3Forms — delivering your form submission to our email
  • Stripe — processing payments
  • Meta — advertising measurement via the pixel (unless you've opted out)

Your choices

  • You can browse the site without giving us any personal information — just don't submit the form.
  • Turn on Global Privacy Control and we won't store the attribution record or load the Meta pixel.
  • Clear your browser's site data to remove anything stored locally.
  • Email hello@wiresetweb.com to see, correct, or delete any inquiry you've sent us.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above. Questions? hello@wiresetweb.com.
